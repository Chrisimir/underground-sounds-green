Privacy Policy

This policy covers Underground Sounds Publisher, the private tool Underground Sounds Green uses to publish its own videos.

What the tool accesses

  • When an account owner connects YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, the platform issues access tokens that let the tool upload videos to that account.
  • The tool reads only basic account information (account name and ID) to show which account is connected.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, followers or any other people.

How data is stored and used

  • Access tokens are stored only on our own private server and are used only to publish our videos to our own accounts.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (Google Account permissions, Instagram/Facebook app settings, TikTok app permissions).
